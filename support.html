Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
Do I have to record every transaction?
No. Private Ledger asks for an account's current balance now and then, not for individual purchases. Recurring costs like subscriptions and rent are declared once.
Why does a balance say “Updated 3 months ago”?
Because it was. Balances you enter by hand stay as entered until you update them — the label is there so an old figure is never mistaken for a current one.
Why do “Last Month” and “Last Year” say “Needs history”?
A percentage needs something to measure from. Until there is a balance recorded at the start of that period, the app shows nothing rather than inventing a number.
My foreign-currency account looks wrong.
Check Settings for a “Missing rates” warning. Without an exchange rate, a currency is counted one-to-one against your base currency. Adding the rate fixes the totals.
Crypto balances aren't updating.
Balances refresh when you open the app, if more than six hours have passed, and you can force it from Settings or the account screen. Only Ethereum and Optimism addresses are supported, and only a fixed list of major tokens.
Can I get my data out?
Not yet. Export is planned — if you need it, say so by email and it will move up.
