Privacy Policy — Private Ledger (MyCapital)

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Private Ledger is a personal net-worth tracker. It is built so that your

financial data stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer operates no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no

advertising. No information about you or your finances is transmitted to us,

because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

What stays on your device

Your accounts, balances, balance history, recurring commitments, cash flows and

exchange rates are stored only in the app's local database on your device. They

are included in your device backup if you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local

backups enabled — that backup is controlled by Apple and by you, not by us.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the app sends to third parties

To show live values, the app makes requests directly from your device to the

following services. It sends only what each request needs, and it never sends

your account names, balances or history to any of them.

CoinGecko

The app sends coin identifiers (for example ethereum, bitcoin) and a

currency code to api.coingecko.com to obtain prices, so that holdings recorded

in a cryptocurrency can be shown in your base currency. No personal information

is included.

Data provided by CoinGecko — https://www.coingecko.com

ExchangeRate-API

The app sends a currency code (for example USD) to open.er-api.com to

obtain exchange rates. No personal information is included.

Rates By Exchange Rate API — https://www.exchangerate-api.com

Tracking

The app does not track you. It contains no advertising identifiers, no

third-party SDKs, no analytics, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any

kind.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
